Quick concept review

  • MAX wants larger utility values.
  • MIN wants smaller utility values.
  • Alpha records MAX’s best guaranteed value so far.
  • Beta records MIN’s best guaranteed value so far.
The search can stop exploring a branch when α ≥ β.

How scoring works

Each card is a MIN branch. Its two numbers are terminal utilities examined from left to right.

  • MIN keeps the smaller value.
  • MAX updates alpha after each branch.
  • A later MIN branch is cut off as soon as β ≤ α.
Your target is to prune at least three leaves.
